An older idea

The commons were never just land.

The Charter of the Forest of 1217 protected customary rights to shared resources against enclosure by power. Civic Commons carries that principle into public knowledge: useful local records, reporting and memory should remain accessible, connected and held in common.

A network, not a franchise

Built locally. Connected openly.

The first Civic Commons is in Ealing. The wider domain is here for other local Commons as the model develops — each rooted in its own place, sources and civic life.

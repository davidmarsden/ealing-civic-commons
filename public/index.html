About this experiment

Not another social network.

The Civic Commons is an experiment in connecting information that is already being published independently. Sources keep their own websites and identities. Every item links back to the original.

Southall is the starting point. A borough-wide service would recognise Acton, Ealing, Greenford, Hanwell, Northolt, Perivale and Southall as distinct civic places rather than treating the borough as one interchangeable audience.

This is an early read-only prototype. Topic and place classification is deliberately simple and may be wrong; provenance is kept visible so mistakes can be found and corrected.

Open social layer

From reading to participation.

The Commons now supports stable item pages, moderated contributions, browser-local following, portable personal RSS feeds and account-free email alerts.

RSS remains the most open output: read the short guide. Email is an optional delivery layer over the same stable follows, with double opt-in, one-click unsubscribe and no engagement tracking.

About this experiment

Open civic infrastructure.

The Civic Commons connects information that is already being published independently. Sources keep their own websites and identities. Every item links back to the original.

Southall is the starting point. A borough-wide service recognises Acton, Ealing, Greenford, Hanwell, Northolt, Perivale and Southall as distinct civic places.

Topic and place classification is deliberately simple and may be wrong; provenance is kept visible so mistakes can be found and corrected.

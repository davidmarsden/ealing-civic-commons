# Ealing Civic Commons

![Ealing oak](https://ealing.civiccommons.co.uk/brand/ealing-oak-approved.webp)

**Open civic infrastructure, not a publication.**

Ealing Civic Commons connects civic information that is already being published independently across the London Borough of Ealing: local journalism, community organisations, campaigns, and official democratic records. It covers Acton, Ealing, Greenford, Hanwell, Northolt, Perivale and Southall as distinct civic places.

- **Sources keep their own websites and identities.** Every item links back to the original, which remains canonical.
- **Provenance stays visible.** Topic and place classification is deliberately simple and may be wrong, so mistakes can be found and corrected.
- **Civic memory.** Places, issues, people, organisations and planning records connect items over time.
- **Conversations.** [Commons Chat](https://chat-dev.ealing.civiccommons.co.uk/) is an open, RSS-based conversation space linked to the civic record.

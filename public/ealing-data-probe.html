How to read this

Evidence, not editorial.

Southall town is treated as six wards: Dormers Wells, Lady Margaret, Norwood Green, Southall Broadway, Southall Green and Southall West. Hanwell Broadway, Northfield and Walpole are shown separately as Ealing Southall parliamentary-constituency context, not folded into Southall town statistics.

LSOA values use a human anchor — their ward name — while keeping the official LSOA label and code available for source checking. Comparator figures are calculated at the same geography across Ealing rather than mixing incomparable areas.

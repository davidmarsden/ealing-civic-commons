# Civic Commons brand hierarchy

The brand system separates the network from each local Commons.

## Network mark

**Civic Commons** uses the concentric **CC** monogram.

Use it for:
- `civiccommons.co.uk`;
- network-wide documentation and social cards;
- future multi-borough material.

It must not imply ownership by any one borough or publisher.

## Ealing mark

**Ealing Civic Commons** uses the **oak**.

The oak is a place identity for the London Borough of Ealing, not the global Civic Commons logo. It can also remain the fallback mark for town-filtered views within the Ealing Commons until distinct town marks are introduced.

Use it for:
- `ealing.civiccommons.co.uk`;
- the Southall-focused doorway at `commons.southallstories.uk` while it remains a view onto Ealing Civic Commons;
- Ealing Civic Commons favicons and social sharing cards.

## Future town marks

Town marks may later sit below the Ealing oak in the hierarchy — for example a simplified local civic landmark for Southall, Hanwell or Perivale. They should form one consistent icon family and represent place, not separate data silos or brands.

## Principle

**CC = the Commons network. Oak = Ealing. Future local icons = town lenses.**

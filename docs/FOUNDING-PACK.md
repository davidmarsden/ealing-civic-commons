The Open Social Phase document remains useful as the design rationale for stable items, moderated contributions, follows and portable delivery. Much of its Phase A/B programme is now implemented; current status and priorities belong in the roadmap rather than rewriting the founding record.

## Founding principle

> Publish anywhere. Connect locally. Keep the sources open.

A future borough-wide service should not assume that the priorities of **Ealing, Southall, Greenford, Acton, Northolt, Perivale or Hanwell are interchangeable**.

The Commons is intended to be independent civic infrastructure. No single publisher, council, commercial supplier, social network or data partner should be able to prevent the project from developing.
